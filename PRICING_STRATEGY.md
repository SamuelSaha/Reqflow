# Reqflow Pricing Strategy

**Last Updated:** 2026-03-02
**Status:** Strategic Framework (Currently Free, Future Monetization)
**Market:** Procurement workflow SaaS for small-mid size teams (5-50 people)

---

## Executive Summary

Req flow's pricing strategy is built on **value-based pricing** anchored to **procurement spend managed** and **team size**, with a freemium-to-paid conversion model that emphasizes expansion revenue through feature gating and usage-based upsells.

###Key Strategic Decisions
1. **Primary Value Metric:** Active users (seats) — predictable, scalable, aligns with customer growth
2. **Secondary Metric:** Procurement volume unlocks (spend managed annually) — captures enterprise value
3. **Monetization Model:** Tiered SaaS + usage-based expansion (integrations, automations)
4. **Go-To-Market Motion:** Product-Led Growth (PLG) with sales-assisted for Enterprise

## Value Metrics & Pricing Psychology

### Primary Value Metric: **Per-Seat Pricing**
**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Predictable revenue:** Easy to forecast MRR/ARR
- ✅ **Aligns with customer growth:** As team grows, they pay more
- ✅ **Simple to explain:** "Pay for who uses it"
- ✅ **Industry standard:** Familiar to B2B buyers

**Alternatives Considered:**
- ❌ **Spend-based** ($X per $1M managed): Hard to measure, unpredictable for customer
- ❌ **Request-based** ($X per 1,000 requests): Penalizes power users, discourages adoption
- ❌ **Flat-rate** ($X/month): Leaves money on table at scale, hard to segment

### Pricing Anchors
- **Free:** Establishes "zero-cost" baseline
- **Starter @ $29:** Positions as "affordable" ($290/month for 10-person team)
- **Business @ $49:** 69% premium over Starter → clear value gap
- **Enterprise:** "Custom" = premium positioning, negotiation flexibility

### Psychological Pricing
- **$29** (not $30): Charm pricing reduces friction
- **Annual discount:** 17% off monthly → encourages commitment
- **Most Popular badge:** Business tier (highest expected revenue)

## Expansion Revenue Strategy

### 1. **Seat Expansion** (Primary Growth Driver)
- **Mechanism:** As customer team grows, they add users
- **Trigger:** Department expansion (e.g., HR starts using Reqflow)
- **Revenue Impact:** +20-40% ARR/year from seat growth alone

### 2. **Tier Upgrades** (Conversion Milestones)
- **Free → Starter:** Need integrations (~40% conversion at 5 users)
- **Starter → Business:** Need accounting sync (~25% conversion at 15 users)
- **Business → Enterprise:** Need custom integrations (~10% conversion at 50 users)

### 3. **Add-On Modules** (Future Monetization)
- **Advanced Vendor Management:** +$5/user/month (vendor scorecards, risk assessment)
- **AI Purchase Suggestions:** +$10/user/month (smart recommendations)
- **Procurement Analytics Pack:** +$15/user/month (BI dashboards, forecasting)

### 4. **Overage Charges** (Usage-Based)
- **API calls beyond limit:** $10/1,000 additional calls
- **Storage beyond tier:** $5/10 GB additional
- **Extra workflows (Starter tier):** $50/workflow/month

## Competitive Moats

### Why Customers Will Pay for Reqflow

1. **Time Savings:** Eliminates manual approval chasing ($5K+/year value)

### Pricing Moat
- **Free tier creates lock-in:** Teams won't switch once workflows are built
- **Seat-based growth:** Revenue scales with customer success
- **Annual contracts:** Reduces churn, improves cash flow
